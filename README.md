# Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard

## Project overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to explore retail sales performance and help users examine trends across time, regions, products and customer segments.

The project demonstrates my developing skills in data preparation, business intelligence, data visualisation and communicating analytical findings.

## Business questions

- How do sales change over time?
- Which regions contribute the most to sales?
- Which products perform best?
- How are sales distributed across customer segments?

## Tools and technologies

- **Power BI:** Dashboard development and visualisation
- **Power Query:** Data preparation and transformation
- **DAX:** Measures and calculations
- **Excel:** Source dataset

## Data preparation

The Superstore Excel dataset was used as the source for the dashboard in Power BI. Power Query was used as part of the data preparation process to make the dataset available for analysis and visualisation.

The report uses fields relating to order dates, regions, product categories and customer segments to support comparisons of sales performance.

The specific cleaning operations, including missing-value handling, duplicate removal and data-type corrections, should be documented according to the transformation steps actually performed in Power Query.

## Key findings

1. **Regional performance:** The West region generated the highest total sales, contributing 31% of overall revenue. This suggests that the region was a major contributor to sales performance during the period analysed. Management could investigate the factors behind its performance to identify practices that may be useful in other regions.
   <img width="635" height="340" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3302f773-7423-41de-bd74-e0e59cbb8b90" />

3. **Product performance:** Technology generated the highest sales among the product categories analysed. This indicates that Technology products contributed substantially to sales value. The business could investigate product-level performance and customer demand before making inventory or promotional decisions.
   <img width="445" height="325" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6c436a4f-cefc-4502-8f21-3ab36982e409" />

5. **Sales trends or customer segments:** Sales increased during November compared with the preceding period. This may indicate a seasonal pattern or a change in customer demand. Further analysis would be needed to establish the cause and determine whether the pattern is recurring.
